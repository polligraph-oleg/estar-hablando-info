Help with access

Open Access in the app. A free 7-day trial starts after you confirm its free activation with Apple. After the trial, full access is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. The App Store displays your local price before confirmation.

To restore a purchase, use the same Apple Account and select Restore Purchase on the Access screen. If offers are unavailable, check your connection and select Check again. Redeem eligible offer codes using the app's redemption button on supported iOS versions.

Your saved verbs

Your personal list is stored on your device and remains when the trial expires. Deleting the app can remove locally saved practice data. Purchases are managed by Apple.

Report a problem

Include the app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and the steps that reproduce the issue. Screenshots are useful; remove private information. Never send passwords or payment card details.
